Our Story

The Old Bakery Tearoom was created with one simple idea: to provide a welcoming place where people can enjoy great coffee and spend quality time together.

We believe that visiting a cafe should be more than simply buying a drink. It should be an experience that makes people feel comfortable, relaxed and appreciated.

From our carefully prepared coffees to our delicious selection of snacks, we aim to make every visit special.
